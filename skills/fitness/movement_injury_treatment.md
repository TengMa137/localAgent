# Movement Injury Treatment

## Overview
This skill covers movements and techniques used to treat hurts across various body regions. Each injury type has specific protocols, target areas, and exercises designed to alleviate pain and restore function.

## Categories of Body Parts Treated

### Upper Body Injuries

#### Shoulder Pain
**Target Areas**: Rotator cuff, deltoid, scapular region
**Common Symptoms**: Pain with overhead movement, clicking, weakness
**Protocols**:
1. **Movement-based treatment**: 
   - Scapular retraction and depression exercises
   - External/internal rotation of shoulder girdle
   - Pendulum exercises for glenohumeral mobility
2. **Specific movements**:
   - Band pull-aparts (for external rotator strengthening)
   - Wall slides (for scapular stability)
   - Cross-body raises (for internal rotator activation)

**Benefits**: Reduced pain, improved range of motion, decreased compensatory movements

#### Elbow Pain
**Target Areas**: Humerus, radius, ulna, elbow joint capsule
**Common Symptoms**: Swelling, warmth, limited flexion/extension
**Protocols**:
1. **Movement-based treatment**:
   - Pendulum exercises for soft tissue mobility
   - Wrist flexor/extensor strengthening (proprioceptive)
   - Elbow circles and rotations
2. **Specific movements**:
   - Supinated/pronated wrist circles
   - Wall slides with elbow support
   - Gentle elbow flexion/extension (light weight)

**Benefits**: Reduced inflammation, improved joint stability, better function

#### Neck Pain
**Target Areas**: Cervical spine, cervical paraspinals, SCM
**Common Symptoms**: Stiffness, pain with head turning, radiating symptoms
**Protocols**:
1. **Movement-based treatment**:
   - Cervical flexion/extension (gentle)
   - Side-to-side neck movements
   - Thoracic mobility exercises to compensate
2. **Specific movements**:
   - Neck tilts (up/down) in neutral position
   "W" stretch for rhomboids
   Gentle lateral flexion

**Benefits**: Reduced stiffness, improved cervical posture, decreased strain

### Lower Body Injuries

#### Hip Pain
**Target Areas**: Pelvic floor, gluteal muscles, hip joint capsule
**Common Symptoms**: Swelling, warmth, limited range of motion
**Protocols**:
1. **Movement-based treatment**:
   - Hip abduction/adduction exercises
   - Hip external/internal rotation
   "Bird-dog" for core stability
2. **Specific movements**:
   - Single-leg deadlifts (pain-free range)
   "Clamshells" (for glute medius)
   Glute bridge with hip hinge

**Benefits**: Reduced inflammation, improved movement pattern, decreased compensatory mechanics

#### Knee Pain
**Target Areas**: Quadriceps, hamstrings, patellar tendon, meniscus
**Common Symptoms**: Swelling, warmth, limited range of motion, pain with weight-bearing
**Protocols**:
1. **Movement-based treatment**:
   - Gentle quadriceps activation (straight leg raise)
   - Hamstring lengthening (eccentric)
   Knee flexion/extension mobility
2. **Specific movements**:
   - Wall slides (with knee support)
   "Clamshells" (if no hip pain)
   Quadriceps sets with light resistance

**Benefits**: Reduced swelling, improved knee mechanics, better function

#### Ankle Pain
**Target Areas**: Tibia, fibula, ankle joint capsule, plantar fascia
**Common Symptoms**: Swelling, warmth, limited range of motion, instability
**Protocols**:
1. **Movement-based treatment**:
   - Ankle pumps (for circulation)
   - Dorsiflexion/plantarflexion mobility
   Heel-toe walks
2. **Specific movements**:
   - Towel scrunches (for intrinsic foot muscles)
   Ankle circles (various directions)
   Step-ups with heel elevation

**Benefits**: Reduced swelling, improved circulation, better proprioception

### Chest and Abdominal Injuries

#### Chest Pain
**Target Areas**: Pectorals, intercostal muscles, diaphragm
**Common Symptoms**: Pain with chest movement, breathing difficulty
**Protocols**:
1. **Movement-based treatment**:
   - Deep breathing (diaphragmatic)
   Thoracic rotations and extensions
   Scapular retraction
2. **Specific movements**:
   "W" stretch for pecs
   Chest expansion on exhalation
   Diaphragmatic rises

**Benefits**: Improved breathing mechanics, reduced chest wall pain

#### Abdominal Pain
**Target Areas**: Rectus abdominis, obliques, transverse abdominis
**Common Symptoms**: Swelling, warmth, limited range of motion
**Protocols**:
1. **Movement-based treatment**:
   - Deep diaphragmatic breathing
   Transverse abdominis activation
   Pelvic floor engagement
2. **Specific movements**:
   "Dead bug" for core stability
   Plank variations
   Cat-cow for thoracic mobility

**Benefits**: Improved intra-abdominal pressure, better posture, reduced strain

### Back Injuries

#### Lower Back Pain
**Target Areas**: Lumbar vertebrae, erector spinae, gluteals, hamstrings
**Common Symptoms**: Pain with sitting, standing, walking; radiating symptoms
**Protocols**:
1. **Movement-based treatment**:
   - Gentle lumbar extension (if no nerve pain)
   Hip flexor lengthening
   Hamstring strengthening
2. **Specific movements**:
   "L" stretch for hamstrings
   Glute bridge with hip hinge
   Bird-dog for core stability

**Benefits**: Reduced lower back strain, improved posture, decreased radiating symptoms

#### Upper Back Pain
**Target Areas**: Rhomboids, trapezius, levator scapulae
**Common Symptoms**: Stiffness, pain with overhead movement, rounded shoulders
**Protocols**:
1. **Movement-based treatment**:
   - Shoulder shrugs (if no neck pain)
   Upper back extension
   Thoracic mobility exercises
2. **Specific movements**:
   "Y" stretch for rhomboids
   Scapular retraction and depression
   Thoracic rotations

**Benefits**: Improved posture, reduced upper back strain, decreased compensatory movements

## General Treatment Principles

### 1. Movement-Based Approach
- Prioritize movement over static stretching
- Use specific exercises that address the underlying dysfunction
- Focus on restoring proper biomechanics

### 2. Targeted Protocols
- Each injury has a unique set of movements based on anatomical location
- Adaptations available for different fitness levels and equipment availability
- Progressive loading to promote healing while maintaining function

### 3. Benefits Summary
- Reduced pain and inflammation
- Improved range of motion
- Better joint mechanics and stability
- Decreased compensatory movement patterns
- Enhanced functional capacity

## Safety Considerations

### Pre-Treatment Assessment Required
- Medical clearance before starting treatment exercises
- Identify specific injury type and severity
- Assess for contraindications (e.g., acute inflammation, fractures)

### Exercise Modifications by Injury Type
- **Acute phase**: Focus on pain-free range of motion only
- **Subacute phase**: Introduce strengthening movements with light loads
- **Remission phase**: Add functional movement patterns

## Documentation
All treatment protocols should be documented in the user's health record. Track:
- Exercises performed (type, sets, reps)
- Pain levels before and after each session
- Progressions made
- Any modifications needed
